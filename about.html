About the firm

Independent legal thinking. Practical representation.

Alphonce Odhiambo & Company Advocates is a Kenyan legal practice based in Kisumu, with a professional profile rooted in advocacy, legal analysis and research.

The practice

A direct, research-led approach to legal work.

The firm is led by Alphonce Odhiambo, an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya. His public professional profile describes him as a legal researcher and legal analyst and records membership in the Law Society of Kenya and the East African Law Society.

The firm’s working method is deliberately clear: understand the facts, identify the controlling law, define the client’s objective, then choose the most effective next step—whether advice, negotiation, written opinion, court action or another dispute-resolution route.
