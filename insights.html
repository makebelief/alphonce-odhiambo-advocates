Insights

Law, rights and the practical consequences of legal rules.

Short website summaries inspired by themes appearing in Alphonce Odhiambo’s public legal research. They are general information, not legal advice.

Administrative law

Fair administrative action is a practical right

When public or administrative decisions affect rights and interests, the law may require lawful authority, fair process, reasons and an opportunity to respond. The exact remedy depends on the decision, the procedure followed and the governing statute.

Criminal procedure

Bail decisions engage constitutional liberty

Bail is not merely a scheduling issue. It sits at the intersection of liberty, the presumption of innocence, attendance at trial and any legally established reasons for limiting release.

Research profile →
Dispute resolution

Litigation still has a central role

Negotiation, mediation and other ADR mechanisms can resolve many disputes efficiently, but some controversies require a court to determine rights, apply precedent or grant coercive remedies.

Research profile →
Family law

Family rules can carry constitutional consequences

Questions around marriage, children, custom and family arrangements can engage dignity, equality, personal autonomy and the best interests of the child.

Research profile →
Children's rights

The best-interests principle requires substance

Children’s legal rights should be considered through the concrete effect of decisions on their welfare rather than through labels of charity, convenience or tradition.

Research changes the quality of legal strategy

Where a legal problem crosses statutes, constitutional provisions and developing case law, disciplined research helps identify stronger arguments and avoid assumptions.
